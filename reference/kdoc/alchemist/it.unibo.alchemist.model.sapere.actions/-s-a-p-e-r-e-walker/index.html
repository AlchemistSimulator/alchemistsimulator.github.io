SAPEREWalker

class SAPEREWalker(environment: MapEnvironment<List<ILsaMolecule>, GraphHopperOptions, GraphHopperRoutingService>, reaction: NodeReaction<List<ILsaMolecule>>, tag: ILsaMolecule, speed: Double, interaction: Double, range: Double) : MoveOnMap<List<ILsaMolecule>, GraphHopperOptions, GraphHopperRoutingService> , ILsaAction

Follows a GPS trace on the streets, slowing down by the interaction factor near the nodes tagged with tag within range. The reaction is used to compute the movement length, the node walks at an average speed.
