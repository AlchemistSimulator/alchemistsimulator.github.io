Reaction

interface Reaction<T> : Comparable<Reaction<T>> , Disposable

A scheduled entity whose absolute occurrence time is owned by the entity itself.

The engine initializes a reaction before scheduling it, indexes nextOccurrence, and subscribes to that observable without replaying its current value. After the scheduler selects a finite occurrence, the engine calls execute. Observed model inputs synchronously mark the reaction dirty. When the enclosing model mutation completes, the engine invokes updateSchedulingAfterInvalidation once; the implementation owns that policy and communicates scheduling changes only by emitting a new nextOccurrence.
