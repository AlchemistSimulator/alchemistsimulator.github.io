LsaStandardCondition

open class LsaStandardCondition(molecule: ILsaMolecule, reaction: NodeReaction<List<ILsaMolecule>>) : AbstractLsaCondition

Simple LSA condition (example: <grad, X, 1>), searching an instance of the molecule template in the node. The matched LSA, if any, is not deleted from the LSA space of the node, although the reaction may do so.
