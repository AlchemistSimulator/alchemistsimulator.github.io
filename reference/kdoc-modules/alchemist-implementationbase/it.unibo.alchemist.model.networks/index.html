A RangeBasedNetworkModel where each node has its own range, adjusted to keep its number of neighbors close to a target: two nodes are linked when each is within the range of the other, and conditionForRemoval does not hold. Whenever its neighborhood changes, the range of a node converges, by steps of adjustment within minRange and maxRange, until its number of neighbors is within tolerance of desiredNeighbors.

ObservingRangeBasedNetworkModel
Link copied to clipboard
abstract class ObservingRangeBasedNetworkModel<T, P : Position<P>, Observed>(environment: Environment<T, P>, range: Double) : RangeBasedNetworkModel<T, P>

A RangeBasedNetworkModel whose links depend on the state of the nodes, such as their positions or contents: each node exposes it as an Observed, through observeInput, and whether two nodes are linked is a function of their inputs only. Neighborhoods are recomputed whenever the candidate neighbors, by default the nodes within range, change, or the input of the center or of any candidate does.

RangeBasedNetworkModel

abstract class RangeBasedNetworkModel<T, P : Position<P>>(environment: Environment<T, P>, range: Double) : NetworkModel<T>

A NetworkModel linking each node of environment to the nodes within range of it for which isLinked holds. Neighborhoods follow the nodes within range: isLinked must depend on nothing that changes while they stay in range, models whose links depend on further state extend ObservingRangeBasedNetworkModel instead.
