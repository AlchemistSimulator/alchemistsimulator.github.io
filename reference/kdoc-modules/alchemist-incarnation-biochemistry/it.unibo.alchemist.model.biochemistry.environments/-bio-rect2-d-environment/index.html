BioRect2DEnvironment

open class BioRect2DEnvironment(incarnation: BiochemistryIncarnation, minX: Double, maxX: Double, minY: Double, maxY: Double) : AbstractLimitedContinuous2D<Double> (source)

A rectangular environment for cells, bounded by minX, maxX, minY, and maxY. Inconsistent bounds (a maximum not greater than its minimum) fall back to -1 and 1 for all bounds.
